## Case J12: The customer list gets slower and slower

**Interview prompt.** Hi, let's do an incident scenario. Last week a teammate pinged you: "The customer list page in the admin tool is getting slower every month." At first nobody worried, because it still opened. Today the page took almost a minute to respond, and then the service restarted with an `OutOfMemoryError`. The customer table has just reached about 500,000 rows. The same page was fine when the project started with a few hundred customers. Support is now complaining that the admin tool is unusable, and the alert channel is full of restarts. You are on call. Please walk me through how you would investigate and explain it to your team.

**Follow-up questions**
1. How did you prove it was the cause? What would you check in the logs or in a test?
2. What would you monitor or test afterwards so you notice a similar problem earlier?
3. What is the trade-off of your fix? Could it change behavior for other parts of the project?

**Key vocabulary**
- heap memory / heap dump
- `OutOfMemoryError`
- to degrade over time (performance degradation)
- data growth / scalability
- response time / latency
- to profile the application
- to reproduce with production-sized data
- capacity planning

## Case P24: The user was logged out in the middle of a long form

**Interview prompt.** Hi, here is a ticket that came in from the support team this morning. A user was filling in a long form on our web app, which takes maybe twenty or thirty minutes, with a lot of text fields. When they finally clicked "Submit", they were suddenly logged out and sent back to the login page. Everything they typed was gone. They were very upset and said it was the second time this week. Support cannot reproduce it with short tests, only people who spend a long time on the page seem to hit it. The product owner asks: "Is this a bug or expected behavior? How do we stop losing user data?" You are on call. Please walk me through how you would investigate and explain it to the product owner and your team.

**Follow-up questions**
1. How did you prove it was the cause? What would you check in the logs or in a test?
2. What would you monitor or test afterwards so you notice a similar problem earlier?
3. What is the trade-off of your fix? Could it change behavior for other parts of the project?

**Key vocabulary**
- session / authentication state
- expiry / to expire
- user experience (UX) impact
- to reproduce a time-based issue
- unsaved user input / data loss
- server logs and timestamps
- trade-off between security and usability
- to communicate with a non-technical stakeholder
