## Case M13: Virtual threads, but no throughput gain

**Interview prompt.** Hi, here is a story from our platform team. Last sprint we upgraded our order-search service to Java 21 and switched the request handling to virtual threads. The plan was simple: the service spends most of its time waiting on the database and on a couple of downstream HTTP calls, so everyone expected a big jump in throughput. We ran the same load test as before and, honestly, the numbers barely moved. Requests per second are about the same, and the latency under load looks the same too. CPU is not maxed out, and the database is not the bottleneck according to the DBA. The manager is asking whether the migration was worth it, and the team is a bit confused because every blog post promised much better scaling. You are the engineer who gets this ticket. Please walk me through how you would approach it.

**Follow-up questions**
1. How did you prove it was the cause? What tools or metrics did you use?
2. What would you monitor afterwards to be sure the improvement is real?
3. What is the trade-off of your fix? Does it add risk or complexity?

**Key vocabulary**
- virtual thread / platform thread
- carrier thread
- throughput vs latency
- blocking call
- thread dump / Java Flight Recorder (JFR)
- load test / baseline
- bottleneck

**Speaking challenge:** 90 seconds aloud first. Tip: say "throughput" as /ˈθruːpʊt/ and "latency" as /ˈleɪtənsi/.

## Case M10: Heap grows until we restart every 3 days

**Interview prompt.** Hi, I want to hand you an on-call story from last week. We have a Spring Boot service that has been running fine for months. Recently, the team noticed that the heap usage in our dashboard goes up slowly and steadily. After each garbage collection the "floor" of the memory graph is a little higher than before. After about three days the service becomes very slow, GC runs almost all the time, and then it is close to running out of memory. Right now the on-call engineer simply restarts the service every three days, and that makes everything look healthy again. Nobody has changed the traffic pattern, and there is no sudden spike. This is not sustainable, and the product owner asks you, "Why do we need to restart it, and how do we stop doing that?" Please walk me through your thinking.

**Follow-up questions**
1. How did you prove it was the cause? What evidence would you collect?
2. What would you monitor afterwards, and what alert would you set?
3. What is the trade-off of your fix, and how would you roll it out safely in production?

**Key vocabulary**
- heap usage / garbage collection (GC)
- heap dump
- retained size
- steady growth over time
- out-of-memory error (OOM)
- workaround vs permanent fix
- GC pause

**Speaking challenge:** 90 seconds aloud first. Tip: "heap" is /hiːp/ (long "ee"), and "garbage" is /ˈɡɑːrbɪdʒ/.
